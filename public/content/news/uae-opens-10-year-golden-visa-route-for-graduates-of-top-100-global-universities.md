SEO Meta Title: UAE Golden Visa for Top 100 University Graduates - 10-Year Residency 
Eligible international graduates with a minimum GPA of 3.5 can apply for long-term UAE residency within two years of completing their bachelor's degree. 

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) has introduced a 10-year Golden Visa pathway for graduates of internationally recognised universities ranked among the world's top 100 institutions. The initiative offers eligible international graduates an opportunity to establish their careers in the UAE while benefiting from long-term residency without requiring sponsorship from an employer or a UAE national. 

The pathway could be particularly beneficial for Indian students who have completed their higher education at leading international universities and are considering employment or long-term career opportunities in the UAE. 

## Who Can Apply for the UAE Golden Visa? 

Graduating from a globally recognised university is not sufficient on its own. Applicants must meet specific academic and documentation requirements to qualify under this category. 

The key eligibility criteria include: 

* **University Ranking:** The applicant must hold a bachelor's degree from a university ranked among the world's top 100 institutions under an international ranking recognised by the UAE Ministry of Education. 

* **Minimum GPA:** A cumulative GPA of at least 3.5 out of 4.0, or its equivalent, is required. 

* **Graduation Period:** Applicants must submit their application within two years of their graduation date. 

* **Degree Recognition:** The academic qualification must meet the UAE Ministry of Education's attestation and equivalency requirements. 

## Documents Required for the Application 

Applicants must prepare the necessary academic documents before submitting their Golden Visa application. These include: 

* Attested bachelor's degree certificate. 
* UAE Ministry of Education equivalency certificate. 
* Supporting academic documents, as required by the relevant authorities. 

Applicants should also verify whether their university meets the officially recognised ranking requirements before proceeding. 

## Benefits of the 10-Year UAE Golden Visa 

The Golden Visa provides several advantages for eligible graduates looking to build a future in the UAE. 

Key benefits include: 

* 10-Year Residency: Long-term residence in the UAE with the possibility of renewal. 
* No Local Sponsor: No employer or UAE national sponsorship is required. 
* Career Opportunities: Freedom to live and work in the UAE. 
* Family Sponsorship: Eligible holders can sponsor family members, subject to applicable regulations. 
* Long-Term Stability: An opportunity to plan professional and personal goals in the UAE. 

## What Does This Mean for Indian Students? 

The new pathway creates an additional opportunity for Indian graduates who have studied at leading international universities. Instead of limiting their career options to the country where they completed their education, eligible graduates can also explore the UAE as a potential destination for employment and professional growth. 

However, meeting the university ranking and GPA requirements does not guarantee visa approval. Applications remain subject to document verification and approval by the relevant UAE immigration authorities. 

## Conclusion 

The UAE's 10-year Golden Visa pathway offers an attractive long-term residency option for academically accomplished international graduates. For Indian students planning their international education, university selection and academic performance can play an important role in creating future career and residency opportunities. 

Students interested in studying at globally recognised universities should consider both their academic goals and the career opportunities their qualifications may offer internationally.
