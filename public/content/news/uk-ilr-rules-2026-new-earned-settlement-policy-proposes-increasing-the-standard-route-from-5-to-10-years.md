The United Kingdom has announced major changes to its settlement system, introducing a new approach called **Earned Settlement**. The announcement, made by the UK Home Office on 2 November 2026, could significantly change how international migrants qualify for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR). 

Under the new policy framework, the standard qualifying period for permanent settlement is set to increase from five years to ten years for most migrants. However, individuals who meet specific income, employment and contribution requirements may still qualify through shorter settlement pathways. 

The changes are intended to make permanent settlement dependent not only on the length of time a person has lived in the UK but also on their contribution, character and compliance with immigration requirements. 

## Key Highlights of the UK ILR Update 2026 

Here are the major points from the government's announcement: 

* **Standard settlement period:** Proposed increase from 5 years to 10 years for most migrants. 

* **High earners:** Eligible individuals earning £125,140 or more may qualify after 3 years. 

* **Other higher earners:** Individuals earning at least £50,271 may qualify after 5 years, subject to the required earnings history. 

* **Healthcare and education professionals:** Certain eligible public service workers may retain a 5-year settlement pathway. 

* **Care workers:** Eligible care workers earning at least £25,000 annually and meeting the relevant visa conditions may qualify after 5 years. 

* **Immigration violations:** Certain breaches of immigration rules could result in a settlement waiting period of at least 20 years. 

* **Mandatory conditions:** Applicants will need to meet requirements relating to character, English language, government debt, the Life in the UK Test and sustained contribution. 

## What Is the New Earned Settlement Policy? 

Earned Settlement is the UK government's proposed approach to granting permanent residency. 

Previously, many eligible migrants could apply for ILR after completing five years on qualifying immigration routes, provided they met the relevant requirements. 

Under the revised framework, the standard qualifying period will become ten years. Applicants may be able to reduce this period by demonstrating recognised economic or public service contributions and meeting other eligibility conditions. 

The government's objective is to recognise individuals who contribute to the UK economy and society while maintaining a compliant immigration record. 

## New UK ILR Qualifying Periods Explained 

The announced framework provides different settlement timelines depending on an applicant's circumstances. 

### 1. Three-Year ILR Route - High Earners and Eligible Talent 

Certain high-earning professionals may qualify for settlement after three years. 

This pathway includes individuals earning at least £125,140 annually for three of the four years immediately before their settlement application. 

Eligible individuals under certain high-talent immigration routes, including Global Talent and Innovator Founder, may also benefit from the three-year pathway, subject to the applicable requirements. 

### 2. Five-Year ILR Route - Higher Earners and Public Service Professionals 

Some migrants may continue to qualify for settlement after five years. 

This includes: 

* Individuals earning at least £50,271 annually in three of the five years immediately before applying. 
* Eligible healthcare professionals, including qualifying doctors and nurses. 
* Certain eligible education professionals, including teachers. 
* Care workers who meet the specified conditions, including earning at least £25,000 annually and complying with their visa requirements. 

Not every healthcare or education occupation automatically qualifies. Applicants must meet the relevant occupational and employment conditions. 

### 3. Ten-Year ILR Route - Standard Settlement Pathway 

For most migrants who do not qualify for an accelerated settlement route, the standard qualifying period will increase from five to ten years. 

Applicants will also need to satisfy the relevant character, contribution, financial and integration requirements. 

This means that simply completing ten years of residence may not be sufficient to guarantee settlement. 

### 4. Twenty-Year or Longer Settlement Period 

The government has also introduced longer settlement periods for certain categories of migrants. 

Individuals who have breached immigration conditions or fall under specified circumstances involving public funds may face a settlement period of at least twenty years. 

Some individuals who entered the UK through unauthorised or clandestine routes may face additional settlement restrictions, potentially extending the waiting period to thirty years in certain circumstances. 

These provisions will depend on the individual's immigration history and the detailed rules applicable to their case. 

### What Are the Mandatory Requirements for UK ILR? 

Under the Earned Settlement framework, applicants will generally need to satisfy several mandatory conditions before receiving permanent settlement. 

The announced requirements include: 

**1. Good Character:** Applicants must meet the revised criminality and suitability requirements. 

**2. English Language:** Applicants will need to demonstrate English proficiency at the specified higher standard. 

**3. No Outstanding Government Debt:** Applicants must clear relevant outstanding debts owed to the UK government. 

**4. Sustained National Insurance Contributions:** Applicants must demonstrate the required record of contribution through National Insurance. 

**5. Life in the UK Test:** Applicants must satisfy the applicable Life in the UK Test requirement. 

Certain exemptions may apply to some requirements, but the criminality requirement remains mandatory. 

## Will the New ILR Rules Affect Existing UK Visa Holders? 

Yes, this is one of the most important aspects of the announcement. 

The government has stated that settlement rules will generally be assessed according to the rules applicable at the time of application. This means that people who are already living in the UK should not automatically assume that their previous five-year settlement timeline will remain unchanged. 

The government has also announced targeted transitional protections for certain groups, including eligible care workers and some healthcare and education professionals. 

However, the precise treatment of different existing visa holders will depend on the final Immigration Rules and transitional arrangements. 

Anyone currently working towards ILR should review their immigration category and keep track of further official announcements. 

## What Does the UK ILR Update Mean for International Students? 

The announcement is particularly relevant for international students who are considering the UK for higher education and long-term career opportunities. 

Students should understand that a UK Student visa does not automatically provide a pathway to permanent residency. 

Under the announced framework, time spent on temporary Student and Graduate visas will not generally count towards the proposed earned settlement qualifying period. 

Students planning to build a career in the UK should therefore understand the possible progression from: 

* Student visa 
* Graduate visa, where eligible 
* Skilled Worker visa or another qualifying immigration route 
* Indefinite Leave to Remain, subject to the applicable settlement requirements 

The type of visa held, employment eligibility, salary, occupation and immigration history can all influence long-term settlement options. 

Therefore, choosing a course should involve more than considering university rankings and tuition fees. Students should also research employment opportunities and understand the immigration routes available after graduation. 

## When Will the New UK ILR Policy Be Implemented? 

The UK government announced the Earned Settlement framework on 2 November 2026. 

However, the announcement does not mean that all the revised requirements are already in force. 

The government has stated that the next step is to publish detailed Immigration Rules and operational guidance. The first rule changes are expected in December 2026, with implementation planned during 2027. 

Until the relevant changes come into force, applicants should continue to refer to the Immigration Rules currently applicable to their visa category. 

## Final Thoughts: What Should UK Visa Applicants Do Now? 

The UK's Earned Settlement policy represents a significant change in the country's approach to permanent residency. 

With the proposed standard settlement period increasing from five to ten years, the importance of selecting the right immigration route and maintaining a compliant immigration record will become even greater. 

For international students, skilled professionals and existing UK visa holders, understanding future settlement requirements is essential before making long-term decisions.
